# Houston roofing permits — research (2026-09-24)

No online or instant roofing permit exists in Houston. Residential (HPWCODE1140) and commercial (HPWCODE1157) re-roofs both go through iPermits plus plan review, with a published processing time of 10 business days.

## Residential re-roof — HPWCODE1140
- Source: https://www.houstonpermittingcenter.org/hpwcode1140
- Valid 6 months. Plan review and inspection are required, and the permit must stay on site.
- Exempt: 100 sq ft or less of roof covering (Houston amendment R105.2 item 6.4; media/9056).
- Anyone can apply; no general contractor license is required. Roofer registration is voluntary and needs $500K/$500K liability cover (832-394-8840).
- Documents:
  - CE-1263 application (media/1226)
  - CE-1380 owner declaration (media/2386), checked against HCAD records; a false declaration voids the permit
  - CE-1109 worksheet for a tear-off (media/1801) or CE-1104 for an overlay (media/1841)
- Process:
  1. Create an iPermits account (hpceservices.org).
  2. Email the documents to permits_office@houstontx.gov.
  3. A sales order arrives by email.
  4. Pay online.
- Fee: the 2026 schedule (media/2636) has **no roofing line**. Admin fee $33.56, minimum $91.06, plan review 25%. At the minimum: $91.06 + $22.77 + $33.56 ≈ **$147.39**.
  - UNVERIFIED: which area staff feed into the house square-footage tiers. Tex. Loc. Gov't Code §214.907 bars fees based on value. Confirm with 832-394-8820.
- Inspections: 713-222-9922 (IVR) or 832-394-8840. The CE-1109 items 1–6 are the inspection checklist. CE-1109 covers deck and covering, not rafters or joists.

## Commercial re-roof — HPWCODE1157
- Source: https://www.houstonpermittingcenter.org/hpwcode1157
- Documents: application, plus CE-1076 (commercial worksheet) or CE-1104, plus plans if applicable.
- Process: pay the plan review fee, then upload to ProjectDox. The review can involve many groups.
- Fee by valuation:
  - $47 up to $7,000
  - $47 + $5.36 per extra $1,000 up to $150K
  - minimum $91.06
  - plus 25% plan review and $33.56 admin
- CE-1209 cool roof and insulation rules apply to low-slope tear-offs. 2021 IECC C503.2.1: R-value may not be reduced.

## Code (2021 I-codes + Houston amendments, effective 2024-01-01)
- R908.3: a replacement tears off to the deck.
- R908.3.1.1: no recover over a water-soaked or deteriorated roof, over slate, clay, cement or asbestos-cement tile, or over 2 or more existing layers.
- CE-1104 conditions: sound deck, structure can carry the added load, existing roofing attached, adequate drainage, dry insulation, fire rating kept.
- Houston R905.7: wood shakes are banned in new work; replacements must be fire-retardant-treated wood.
- Wind: speed is address-specific (ASCE 7). Houston is near the 140 mph threshold, where two-layer underlayment and Class H shingles apply. Unverified.
- Rafters and joists need HPWCODE1133 or 1135.
- NOTE: the HPWCODE1140 page still links to the old 2015 IRC amendments.

## Related approvals
- Historic district: no Certificate of Appropriateness for an in-kind re-roof. A material change goes to the Commission (apply 22 days before its meeting). Administrative review takes 15 business days.
- Dumpster in the street (HPWMP1001, GeoLink):
  - up to 10 business days
  - $67.13 per lane, per block, per week on a local street (2026)
  - sidewalk: $80.56 partial, $127.56 full
- HOA: Tex. Prop. Code §202.011 — an HOA can't ban impact-resistant, energy-efficient or solar shingles that look like the neighborhood's.
- The TDI windstorm (WPI-8) area covers no part of the City of Houston.
- Deductibles: Tex. Bus. & Com. Code §27.02. Waiving or rebating one is a Class B misdemeanor, and contracts of $1,000 or more need the notice. Ins. Code §4102.163: a contractor can't act as the public adjuster.

## Pitch facts
- No online roofing permit. After the 2024 storms, HPC's "Fast-Track Your Repair Permit" meant phoning 832-394-8899 with the project number.
- No published roofing permit counts.
- Houston Chronicle: only 3% of residential permits were reviewed within 10 days, against a 90% target. UNVERIFIED (paywall).
- 2024 derecho (May 16): winds of about 100 mph, more than 1M customers without power, 8 deaths, extensive roof damage (City DR24 plan).
- Hurricane Beryl (July 8, 2024):
  - about 2.7M customers lost power
  - City estimate: $2.5–4.5B in damage
  - Verisk: $2–3B in insured wind losses
  - TWIA: 26,605 claims by Aug 5
- Other cities:
  - Los Angeles: Express Permit, issued on payment, no plan check
  - Denver: Quick Permit, no plan review
  - San Antonio: over the counter
  - Austin: express web form, about 1 business day
  - Dallas: online (DallasNow)
